What I have done at this scale

On a contract for a US EdTech company in 2026, two of us ran the whole creator function. The full story is in the case study.

  • 7,000+ creators pitched, about 14% replied, about 800 warm conversations.
  • 45 overnight against a request for 20 expert candidates, for under $3.
  • $1,120 CPM behind a $52k quote, caught before anyone agreed to it.
  • 0.6 to 1.1 times the earlier fee: what past partners asked when invited back.
  • 17 hours → 3 minutes from a teammate's message to a prepared answer.
  • −88% data-collection spend.

How I keep automation safe

  • A person presses Send on every one-to-one message.
  • A person accepts every price, budget and contract term.
  • Addresses are checked before sending, and each mailbox has a daily limit.
  • Official integrations and data you export yourself. I do not scrape behind logins.
  • Every incident becomes a rule in code.
