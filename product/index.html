Tools I built for this work

Two tools sit behind the services. One reads the creator spend you have already made. The other runs outreach with a person approving every send.

Integration audit

It answers one question: in the integrations you already paid for, which content choices went with cheaper contacts, and which did not?

  1. Load your table

    Past integrations with cost and funnel results. Your column names are mapped in a config file, so the export does not need reshaping.

  2. Get the words

    Transcripts and captions for each video, so the analysis reads what the creator actually said.

  3. Score the ad read

    Each integration is scored on fixed criteria, such as how clearly the benefit is stated and how specific the claims are.

  4. Compare inside one platform

    Scores are set against cost per contact. YouTube and short-form video are never pooled, because length, placement and audience intent differ too much.

  5. Report with evidence levels

    Every finding is marked as a signal or a hypothesis, with its sample size and statistical power, followed by a list of what to test next.

Read a sample report. The advertiser, the creators and every number in it are invented.

What it will not tell you

  • It finds associations, not causes. Creators were not given scripts at random.
  • Small samples are flagged as underpowered, not dressed up as findings.
  • Sales that happen several steps after the video are shown as context, not as a verdict on the creative.

Outreach engine

In development.

A new, brand-neutral engine for creator outreach, built on what running 7,000 pitches taught me. The brand's voice, offer and negotiation rules live in a playbook you supply; nothing about your brand is written into the code.

  • Addresses are checked before sending, and each mailbox has a daily limit and a pause switch.
  • Replies are sorted, and each conversation gets one draft: planned from quoted facts, written, then checked.
  • A person presses Send. Every time.
  • Official integrations and lists you export from your own tools. No scraping behind logins.
